How it works

It catches change before it becomes an emergency.

Dynamic charts: the reference band is shown in green, points are colour-coded by status, and the whole crew can be compared.

Personal-baseline drift

Compares your last 3 days with your own 18-day baseline, and flags change even inside the "normal" range.

z = (recent − baseline) / sd · |z| ≥ 2
Multi-signal patterns

Sleep debt + slower reactions → fatigue. Fever + high heart rate + rash → possible infection. Low mood on 3 of 5 days → behavioural health.

Delay-aware escalation

On the ISS: call the flight surgeon. On Mars: follow the onboard protocol now and send a structured downlink report.

NASA Human Research Program

CSA & scientific literature

  • CSA: Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health → cumulative dose tracking
