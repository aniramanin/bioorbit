BioOrbit · Astronaut Health OS
The problem

On Mars, help from Earth
is 22 minutes away.

On long missions there is no doctor on board and no quick way home. Astronauts must notice changes in their own health and decide what to do, often without medical training.

≤ 22 minone-way signal delay between Mars and Earth: a question and its answer can take most of an hour
0medical evacuations possible during a Mars transit or surface stay
