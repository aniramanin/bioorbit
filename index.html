Features

Everything a crew needs to care for itself.

Designed with the questions NASA asks: which measurements can be done in space, what they mean, and when to call the flight surgeon.

Interactive body map

See your whole body at a glance.

Eight body systems, each colour-coded. Tap any of them to learn what it does, what spaceflight changes and what to do right now.

Camera pulse

A heart-rate monitor in any phone.

Put a fingertip on the camera. BioOrbit reads the tiny colour change of every heartbeat, and the image never leaves the device.

72 bpm
Personal baselines

Catches change before it's a problem.

Every value is compared with your own baseline, so a slow drift gets flagged while it's still in the "normal" range.

Reaction test

Fatigue screening from the ISS.

A 3-minute vigilance test (PVT-B) measures reaction time and lapses, which are early signs of sleep loss.

287 ms
Delay-aware guidance

Act first. Earth confirms later.

Guidance and escalation adapt to the communication delay of the mission, from under 1 second on the ISS to 22 minutes on Mars.

🛰🌍
Gamified routine

Healthy habits that stick.

Timed prompts, XP, streaks and badges keep daily measurements part of the mission routine.

How it works

From a measurement to a decision in seconds.

01

Measure

Log vitals, sleep, mood and symptoms, or measure them with the camera pulse and reaction test. Every field explains how to measure it in space.

02

Understand

Values are classified against reference ranges and your personal baseline. Signals are combined into insights such as fatigue or possible infection.

03

Act

Each finding comes with clear onboard actions and a readiness score with duty status, so the crew knows what to do right now.

04

Downlink

A compact, structured report goes to the flight surgeon through any data link, and the app shows when it will arrive.

Built on NASA science

The five hazards of human spaceflight.

NASA's Human Research Program maps five hazards. Every indicator in BioOrbit is linked to at least one of them, and the in-app library explains what to monitor and the countermeasures for each.

NASA HRP hazards ↗
  • ☢
    Space radiationDose tracking vs. the 600 mSv career limit
  • ◉
    Isolation & confinementMood, stress, sleep and reaction time
  • ↔
    Distance from EarthAutonomous, delay-aware decisions
  • ⇵
    Gravity fieldsHeart, bone, muscle and vision (SANS)
  • ⌂
    Hostile closed environmentsSpO₂, breathing, temperature and infection
